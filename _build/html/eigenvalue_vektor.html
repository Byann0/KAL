Eigenvalue dan Eigenvector#

Diketahui matriks:

\[\begin{split} A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

1. Mencari Eigenvalue#

Gunakan persamaan karakteristik:

\[ \det(A-\lambda I)=0 \]
\[\begin{split} \begin{vmatrix} 2-\lambda & 1 \\ 1 & 2-\lambda \end{vmatrix}=0 \end{split}\]

Hitung determinan:

\[ (2-\lambda)(2-\lambda)-1=0 \]
\[ (2-\lambda)^2-1=0 \]
\[ 4-4\lambda+\lambda^2-1=0 \]
\[ \lambda^2-4\lambda+3=0 \]

Faktorkan:

\[ (\lambda-3)(\lambda-1)=0 \]

Maka diperoleh eigenvalue:

\[ \lambda_1=3 \]
\[ \lambda_2=1 \]

2. Mencari Eigenvector#

Eigenvector untuk (\lambda = 3)#

Gunakan:

\[ (A-3I)v=0 \]
\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 2-3 & 1 \\ 1 & 2-3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Misalkan:

\[\begin{split} v= \begin{bmatrix} x\\ y \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Maka:

\[ -x+y=0 \]
\[ y=x \]

Ambil:

\[ x=1 \]

Sehingga:

\[\begin{split} v_1= \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Normalisasi:

\[ ||v_1||=\sqrt{1^2+1^2}=\sqrt{2} \]
\[\begin{split} q_1= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Eigenvector untuk (\lambda = 1)#

Gunakan:

\[ (A-I)v=0 \]
\[\begin{split} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Persamaan:

\[ x+y=0 \]
\[ y=-x \]

Ambil:

\[ x=1 \]

Sehingga:

\[\begin{split} v_2= \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ -1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Normalisasi:

\[ ||v_2||=\sqrt{1^2+(-1)^2}=\sqrt{2} \]
\[\begin{split} q_2= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ -1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

3. Gram-Schmidt#

Matriks:

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Kolom pertama:

\[\begin{split} a_1= \begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Kolom kedua:

\[\begin{split} a_2= \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 1: Vector Pertama#

Normalisasi (a_1):

\[ ||a_1||=\sqrt{2^2+1^2} =\sqrt{5} \]
\[\begin{split} q_1= \frac{a_1}{||a_1||} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Langkah 2: Vector Kedua#

Hitung proyeksi (a_2) terhadap (q_1):

\[\begin{split} a_2 \cdot q_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt5} \begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[ = \frac{2+2}{\sqrt5} = \frac{4}{\sqrt5} \]

Proyeksi:

\[\begin{split} (a_2 \cdot q_1)q_1 = \frac45 \begin{bmatrix} 2\\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 8/5\\ 4/5 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Cari vector tegak lurus:

\[ v_2 = a_2-(a_2 \cdot q_1)q_1 \]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} 1\\ 2 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 8/5\\ 4/5 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = \begin{bmatrix} -3/5\\ 6/5 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Norm:

\[ ||v_2|| = \sqrt{ \left(-\frac35\right)^2+ \left(\frac65\right)^2 } \]
\[ = \sqrt{ \frac9{25}+\frac{36}{25} } = \sqrt{\frac{45}{25}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt5} \]

Normalisasi:

\[\begin{split} q_2= \frac{v_2}{||v_2||} = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac1{\sqrt5}\\ \frac2{\sqrt5} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

4. Python Code#

import numpy as np

# Matriks
A = np.array([
    [2, 1],
    [1, 2]
])

# =========================
# Eigenvalue & Eigenvector
# =========================
eigenvalues, eigenvectors = np.linalg.eig(A)

print("Matriks A:")
print(A)

print("\nEigenvalue:")
print(eigenvalues)

print("\nEigenvector:")
print(eigenvectors)

# =========================
# Gram-Schmidt
# =========================

# Ambil kolom matriks
a1 = A[:, 0]
a2 = A[:, 1]

# Langkah 1
q1 = a1 / np.linalg.norm(a1)

# Langkah 2
proj = np.dot(a2, q1) * q1
v2 = a2 - proj
q2 = v2 / np.linalg.norm(v2)

print("\nq1:")
print(q1)

print("\nProyeksi a2 ke q1:")
print(proj)

print("\nv2:")
print(v2)

print("\nq2:")
print(q2)

# Matriks Q
Q = np.column_stack((q1, q2))

print("\nMatriks Q:")
print(Q)
